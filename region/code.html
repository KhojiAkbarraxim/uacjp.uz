Our Mission

The Kanto chapter connects leading Uzbek scholars, researchers, and students across Tokyo, Yokohama, and surrounding prefectures. We foster a collaborative environment that bridges Japanese technological innovation with academic rigor, creating pathways for high-impact research and cross-cultural dialogue.
